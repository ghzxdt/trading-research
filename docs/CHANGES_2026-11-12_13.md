## 5. Tracking whether we are working on the right things

| Change | Why |
|---|---|
| **Journal**, `thesis/journal.py`, `data/thesis/journal.jsonl` | Call-outs that aren't trades, each saved with what the page showed and later shown with the next 5 and 20 sessions. Append-only |
| **Idea ledger**, `thesis/ledger.py`, `data/thesis/ideas.jsonl` | Every idea from "idea" to "kept" or "dropped", counting everything tested. Seeded with 11 ideas; ideas 2 and 9 moved to "forward" when the pre-close log started |
| "Leans on" tags on calls, with a by-tag table once calls are scored | To see which inputs go with good calls |
| **Paper ticket and paper book**, `thesis/paper_book.py` | User asked for a printed ticket of trade ideas. Each logged long is followed to its rule's own exit (the stance log scores a fixed 5 sessions instead), with the backtest's average beside the result. It is computed from the stance log, so it adds no new log. It reads both apps: the logs here and the backtester's evidence |
| **Pre-close check**, `thesis/preclose.py`, `data/thesis/preclose.jsonl` | Buying at the signal close tested better than the next open for every rule. The check runs at about 3:50 PM ET, before the close, is compared later with the real close (flip rate), and opens paper trades at that close for close-entry Leads. It refuses to log when yesterday's prices are missing |
